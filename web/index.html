Questions

Before you buy

Do I need a Google Business Profile already?

No. If you don't have one, the Growth and Pro plans include creating and verifying it. On Starter, you can add the profile setup add-on.

How do WhatsApp messages get sent?

Messages go from your own WhatsApp number. The app opens each chat with the message ready, so you tap send. The Pro plan can add the official WhatsApp Business API for one-click bulk sending. Meta charges its own per-message fees for that.

Is my customer data safe?

Your customer list is private to your account. We never message your customers ourselves unless you ask us to, and you can export or delete your data at any time.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next billing date. Yearly plans are paid upfront and get two months free.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new business gets 3 days free with everything unlocked. Pay by UPI any time during the trial to keep going. If you don't pay, your account turns read-only and your data stays safe.

How do I pay?

Pay by UPI from any app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM). You'll get an invoice on WhatsApp and email after we confirm the payment.
