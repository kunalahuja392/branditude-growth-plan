Questions owners ask us

Can I buy just one product?

Yes. Review & Feedback, Loyalty Card, Staff Attendance and QR Menu are each sold on their own. You can add more later or move to a plan any time.

Is there a contract?

No. Pay monthly and stop any month, or pay yearly and get 2 months free.

Who sets it up?

We do, with you. Within 48 hours we add your logo, menu, staff and rewards, and send you QR codes to print.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Customers scan a QR and everything opens in their phone's browser.

How do WhatsApp messages work?

Reminders and offers go out on WhatsApp from a verified business number. Messages use WhatsApp credits, which you top up in the app.

How do I pay?

Online through Razorpay: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit or credit card, netbanking or wallets. It switches on as soon as you pay.

Is my customer data safe?

Your data is private to your business. We never message your customers unless you ask, and you can export or delete it any time.

Not sure what you need?

Tell us about your business on WhatsApp. We'll suggest the right product or plan and set it up with you. We usually reply within an hour in working hours.
