Questions from restaurant owners

Is this like Swiggy or Zomato?

It's your own ordering app, not a marketplace. Customers come to it from your link, QR codes, Instagram, Google profile and WhatsApp. You don't pay commission on the orders.

Who delivers the food?

Porter. When an order is ready, a rider is booked automatically and the customer gets a live tracking link. You don't need your own delivery staff.

What does delivery cost?

Porter's charge for each trip, as actual, depending on distance. You can add a delivery fee to the customer's bill to cover it.

How do customers pay?

UPI, cards or netbanking at checkout through Razorpay, or cash on delivery if you switch it on. Payment gateway charges are as actual.

What does ₹1,499 a month include?

Your ordering app with your menu and branding, the order screen for your phone or tablet, automatic Porter booking, WhatsApp order updates and your customer list. Bigger setups may cost more; we'll confirm on the demo.

When can I start?

We're setting up restaurants one by one. Book a demo and we'll give you your start date and help you load your menu.
